# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Mark One.

This repository doesn't accept pull requests; they are closed automatically. To report a bug, suggest a feature or propose a change, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Sbonelo-Mntungwa/mark-one/issues/new). Include what you expected, what happened, and, for a circuit, the design (Experiment designs are saved in `mark_one_save.json`).

You are welcome to fork the project under the terms of the [MIT license](LICENSE).
